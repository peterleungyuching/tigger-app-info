Privacy policy

This policy covers ai project, the Google OAuth application used by Tigger, Peter Leung's personal assistant. Peter Leung operates the app for personal use. Hermes is its runtime. The public information pages are not a public assistant service and do not expose the owner's connected data.

Data and permissions

Google's OAuth consent process supplies access and refresh credentials and the scopes granted by the account owner. The app does not ask for the Google account password.

  • Calendar: calendar lists and event information, which can include titles, times, descriptions, locations, availability and attendee information. The existing Calendar scope permits reading and managing events. The assistant uses it for the owner's scheduling requests, subject to instructions and confirmation requirements for actions such as invitations or deletion.
  • Gmail read-only: relevant message and thread metadata and content, such as sender, recipient, subject, dates and message body, for requested search, summaries and follow-up identification. The grant is read-only and does not permit Gmail sending or modification.
  • Assistant records: messages, requested outputs, selected summaries, project notes and operational logs may include information obtained from these services.

No connected account content, credential or private board is part of this public website. New services or broader scopes require a separate authorization decision; a missing unrelated scope is not a reason to request all Google services.

Purpose and limits

Google data is used to provide the owner's requested personal-assistant functions: understand schedules, find conflicts, prepare reminders, summarise important mail and record relevant actions in the owner's chosen private workspaces. Data is not sold or used by this app for advertising, credit decisions or data-broker activity.

The operator does not use Google data to train general-purpose AI models. Processing Google data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. This is not a claim that Google has verified the app or audited every downstream service.

AI summaries and recommendations can be wrong. The owner should review them before acting. The app is not a financial, medical or legal decision service.

Services that process data

The assistant is not local-only. Relevant content can be transmitted when necessary to fulfil an authorized request:

  • Google handles authentication and the requested Calendar and Gmail API operations.
  • OpenAI API and configured AI inference providers may receive request context and tool results to generate the assistant's response. Provider retention and account settings apply. OpenAI's API data practices are described in its data controls documentation; this app does not promise zero retention.
  • Honcho / Plastic Labs is a configured conversation-memory service. Relevant messages or derived context may be stored and processed for personalised memory. Its privacy policy describes its retention, subprocessors and permitted processing, including certain de-identified non-public fine-tuning. That is not a promise that all memory data stays on the owner's device or that every provider processing setting has been independently verified.
  • Discord or Weixin / WeChat processes messages and replies when the owner uses those channels. Requested account summaries sent in replies are then subject to that messaging service's handling.
  • Notion and Google Drive may store selected private summaries or project records when the owner directs the assistant to save them there.

The public information host does not receive Calendar or Gmail data from these pages. The operator does not publish connected account data through this site. Service providers may process data in countries outside the owner's country. Do not send sensitive content to a selected processor unless its terms and settings are acceptable for that use.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are kept in the existing credential files on the owner's assistant computer. They are not included in this public site or its repository. API requests use Google's HTTPS endpoints. This policy does not claim that all local files are encrypted at rest.

Assistant conversations, logs, memory and selected notes may be retained in local storage and the services described above. There is no single fixed automatic deletion period configured across all these locations. Owner-controlled notes can remain until the owner removes them; providers have their own retention and backup periods.

Revoking a Google grant stops future use of that grant but does not automatically remove copies already retained in conversations, notes, logs or provider systems. A deletion request must address those locations as well. Copies still in provider backups or subject to legal retention may not disappear immediately.

Revocation, deletion and contact

The owner can review or remove the app under Google Account third-party connections. Refresh access can also stop working after account or policy changes; Production status is not a guarantee of permanent access.

For access, correction, export or deletion requests, contact the operator using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen for ai project. The operator will identify relevant app-controlled records and assist with deletion or the applicable provider controls. Do not put tokens, authorization callbacks or private mail in public issue trackers.

Removing authorization is different from requesting deletion. Be specific about whether the request covers local assistant history, cloud memory, messaging history or saved private notes.

This website and policy changes

These static pages contain no sign-in form, account API, analytics script, advertising, cookie-based tracking or external font loading. GitHub Pages hosts the public files and may process ordinary access metadata such as IP addresses and request logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

The effective date identifies this version. Material changes to how Google data is used must be disclosed before the new use and require any necessary consent. Changing the app's public description does not authorize sharing a private website, inbox or calendar.
