Tigger helps Peter organise his calendar, understand important mail and keep track of work.

Operated by Peter Leung for personal use. Hermes is the underlying assistant runtime. This public information site explains the app; it does not provide access to the assistant, a calendar, an inbox or a task board.

What Google access is for

  • Google Calendar: read calendars and events, check availability and help with requested scheduling. The Calendar permission also allows event changes; changes must follow the owner's instructions and confirmation rules.
  • Gmail read-only: find and read relevant messages, summarise them and identify follow-up actions. This grant does not permit sending, deleting or modifying Gmail messages.

The integration uses Google's consent screen and offline authorization so the local assistant can refresh access without requesting sign-in on every use. Access can still be revoked or expire under Google's rules.

An assistant with clear boundaries

Requested information may be processed by configured AI services and delivered through the owner's messaging channels. Relevant conversations or summaries may be retained in assistant memory, owner-controlled files or a private Notion workspace. The assistant is not a local-only data system.

This website contains no Google sign-in form, connected account data, tracking script or assistant API. Authorizing Google access is a separate action on Google's own consent screen.
